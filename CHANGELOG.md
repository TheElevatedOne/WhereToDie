# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- The Page of Reincarnation no longer drops as a page while "Give Reincarnation with the N’tormat" is on, since the
  N'tormat already gives it (the N'tormat's own random page, the Ancient Sepulcher and the Shrine of Compulsion used to
  give it again). The other pages keep their relative chances.
- Config sections renamed to Indicators, Page fixes and New zones; settings in the old sections go back to their defaults.

## 1.0.0

- Initial release.
- Indicators: green-yellow / green-red colouring of the Reincarnation icon while standing where it works, and a hallowed
  aurora above every such place (stage-wide and sparser during the first 30 seconds of a normal stage).
- Page fixes: the N'tormat gives the Page of Reincarnation; the page recharges on arrival in the Soulbound Threshold.
- New zones: Reincarnation leads to the Soulbound Threshold from the rocketship roof (after Mithrix) and the
  Planetarium's final arena (after Voidling), until Terminal Lucidity is entered.
- After the rocketship roof or the Planetarium, the Soulbound Threshold and Terminal Lucidity lead to Obliteration instead of
  back to the run, and Obliteration has no way back (Beads of Fealty don't lead to A Moment, Whole).
- The Soulbound Threshold can be entered again after the Anima Harvester while Terminal Lucidity hasn't been entered.
- Risk Of Options support and the `wheretodie_status` console command.
